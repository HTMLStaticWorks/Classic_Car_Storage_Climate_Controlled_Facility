Why Regular Oil Changes Are the Secret to Engine Longevity

Jonathan Reed, Lead Mechanic
Oct 12, 2026 • 5 min read

It's the most common piece of advice any mechanic will give you, yet it's often the most ignored: change your oil regularly. Let's explore exactly why this simple service is the backbone of your vehicle's health.

The Core Issue: Friction and Heat

Your engine consists of hundreds of moving parts operating at high speeds. This generates an enormous amount of friction and heat. Motor oil acts as a lubricant, reducing this friction and carrying heat away from the engine block.

Over time, this oil breaks down due to the intense thermal environment. It also collects dirt, metal shavings, and combustion byproducts, turning from a smooth amber fluid into a thick, abrasive sludge.

What This Means for Your Engine

If you skip oil changes, the dirty oil stops lubricating and starts acting like liquid sandpaper, wearing down engine components prematurely. This can lead to catastrophic engine failure.

At Auto Fix, we advise our customers to:

  • Check oil levels monthly using the dipstick.
  • Follow the manufacturer's recommended service intervals.
  • Consider synthetic oil for better extreme-temperature performance and longer lifespan.
