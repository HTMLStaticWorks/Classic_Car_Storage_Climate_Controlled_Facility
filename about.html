Our Foundation

Precision Engineering Meets White-Glove Passion

Collector vehicles require specialized care that standard garages and generic auto freight cannot provide. From temperature fluctuations that compromise seals to road grit that ruins concourse paint, every factor matters.

VaultX was built to set the gold standard: purpose-engineered 50,000 sq ft climate-controlled vaults maintaining exactly 68°F and 50% relative humidity, coupled with a nationwide fleet of enclosed air-ride carriers with hydraulic lift gates.
