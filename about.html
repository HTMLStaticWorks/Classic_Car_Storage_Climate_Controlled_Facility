Our Origins

Passion for Precision

Founded by master mechanics and auto enthusiasts, Auto Fix was built on the principle that true vehicle care requires an intimate understanding of complex automotive engineering.

We saw a gap between dealership prices and corner garage reliability. Our solution was to create a modern, tech-driven auto repair center equipped with advanced diagnostics and a proprietary customer dashboard, bringing transparency and speed to auto maintenance.
