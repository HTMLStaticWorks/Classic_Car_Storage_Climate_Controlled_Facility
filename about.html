Our Preservation Philosophy

Precision Climate Control Meets Custodial Passion

Classic automobiles require dedicated atmospheric stability that standard garages cannot provide. From temperature swings that harden gaskets to ambient humidity that causes hidden chassis corrosion, environmental control is paramount.

VaultX was purpose-built to eliminate deterioration: 50,000 sq ft climate-controlled storage suites maintaining constant 68°F and precision 50% relative humidity, continuous battery float care, and tailored dust covers, supported by white-glove concierge transit.
