Classic Car Storage & Preservation Services

Preserving and protecting the world's most cherished vintage, classic, and exotic automobiles with museum-grade climate control, dedicated vehicle care, and white-glove concierge support.

Service 01

Climate-Controlled Storage Suites

Our purpose-built facilities provide museum-grade preservation environments. We regulate temperature and humidity 24/7/365 to eliminate rust, preserve delicate leather, stabilize gaskets, and prevent paint deterioration.

  • Constant 68°F (20°C) and precision 50% relative humidity regulation
  • Dual HEPA air purification and active carbon filtration systems
  • 24/7 real-time telemetry monitoring with automated environmental alerts
  • Dedicated storage bays with generous vehicle clearance and non-marking epoxy flooring

Service 02

Secure Indoor Storage & Dust Protection

Uncompromising facility security paired with dedicated dust-free vehicle care. Every vehicle is sheltered beneath a custom-fitted, breathable soft satin cover within our biometric-access indoor vaults.

  • Multi-tier biometric access controls and 24/7 high-definition security surveillance
  • Tailored breathable soft satin dust covers preventing atmospheric settling
  • Monitored motion detection, VESDA early laser smoke detection, and dry-pipe suppression
  • Strict custodial access protocols with no unauthorized public entry
Request Facility Walkthrough
Service 03

Battery Care & Active Maintenance Programs

Preserving mechanical integrity is as critical as preserving bodywork. Our certified automotive custodians maintain optimal electrical charge, rotate tires to prevent flat-spotting, and perform routine conditioning.

  • Dedicated CTEK microprocessor float chargers connected to every stored vehicle
  • Scheduled bi-weekly tire roll rotations to eliminate tire flat-spotting
  • Hand wash, paint decon, and museum-grade preservation detailing upon request
  • Fluid level verifications, tire pressure checks, and warm-up cycles per owner specifications
View Care Packages
Service 04

40-Point Condition & Intake Inspection Audits

Every vehicle entering our climate-controlled facility undergoes an exhaustive 40-point photographic condition inspection. All documentation and high-resolution audit scans are securely archived in your collector dashboard.

  • High-resolution 360-degree digital exterior and undercarriage condition documentation
  • Electronic paint depth gauge readings, glass inspection, and cosmetic mapping
  • Fluid integrity audits, battery health diagnostic readouts, and tire tread depth logs
  • Tamper-proof digital timestamping accessible 24/7 via the client portal
